## 4. Sourceability ladder — THREE tiers, and they are not interchangeable

This is the operational payload for the sourceability gate. The same colour can sit in a different tier.

**Tier A — orderable.** Sheet `Can order and new item`: **42 SKUs**, free stock 77 slabs @ 30/04/2024
(`!D57,H57`). Import orders can be placed.

**Tier B — free stock only, CANNOT be ordered.** Sheet `Cannot order`: **19 SKUs**, free stock 7 slabs

**Tier C — clearance / discontinued.** `694483_CS_for_Clear_Mar2024.pdf` (**42 SKUs**, Mar 2024). The
supplier's own instruction, `p.44`, verbatim:
> "สินค้าหมดแล้วหมดเลย กรุณาเช็คสต็อกก่อนนำเสนอลูกค้า **ไม่แนะนำให้วาง spec ในระยะยาว** เนื่องจากไม่สามารถจองสินค้าให้ได้"

**⇒ Spec rule (supplier-stated, `p.44`): do NOT put a Tier-C clearance SKU on a long-term spec.**
Tier B is stock-limited. Only Tier A is safe for a spec that must survive to procurement — and even Tier A
free stock is a 2024 snapshot: re-check before it gates a deliverable. `p.44` additionally routes cut-to-size
enquiries to a named Häfele contact ("ต้องการราคาแบบ cut to size ติดต่อ …", a `@hafele.co.th` address; the
individual is not reproduced here). That is a contact channel only — the per-SKU **CUT TO SIZE** prices *are*
printed in the PDF, on every SKU page, and are transcribed in §7.

## 8. For FF&E / BOM / rate-card

- **Specify by `CS code + colour + thickness + Häfele new article no.`** (e.g. `2141/13 SNOW 13 MM →
  562.60.065`). Colour name alone is not an order key — the same colour exists at two thicknesses with
  different article numbers.
- **Thickness is a design decision with a price step:** 13 → 20 mm costs +2,300 THB (Standard) and
  +2,200 THB (Premium) on cut-to-size; +5,500 / +6,100 THB on slab (§2). 20 mm reads as a chunkier
  countertop edge; the source gives no other guidance on when to use which.
- **DERIVED, not printed — an all-in ฿/m² band for a "Caesarstone-grade quartz countertop".** **Assumption 1
  (both bands):** the cut-to-size column is THB/m² — see the UNIT CAVEAT in §2; the sheet prints no unit.
  On that assumption, adding the **Premium** stonemason install of **7,000 / SQM** (`!F66` — the *only* install
  figure whose per-m² unit is actually printed in the source) gives roughly **฿25,600–71,500 /m²**
  (material 18,600–64,500 + 7,000). A **Standard**-tier band of roughly **฿22,100–68,000 /m²**
  (material 18,600–64,500 + install 3,500) requires a **second, independent assumption**: that the Standard
  install 3,500 is *per m²* — which §3 shows the sheet does **not** print (`!F59` = `3,500 THB/ (Retail Channel)`,
  no unit after the slash). Every figure here is **derived from an expired list price, with an unconfirmed unit
  and unstated VAT** — confirm with Häfele before any of it reaches a client-facing number.
- **Sourceability gating:** only §5 (Tier A) is order-backed. §6 is free-stock-only; §7 is clearance and the
  supplier explicitly says not to spec it long-term (§4). A render that visualises a Tier-B/C colour is
  visualising a spec that may be unbuyable by the time of procurement.
