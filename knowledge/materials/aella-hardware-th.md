## For FF&E / BOM
- Cabinet/drawer pulls (L-series) 210–990 THB and square handles 230–650 THB are the budget-workhorse lines;
  lever door sets (A-series) 4,500–13,000 THB and long designer bar pulls are the premium lines.
- **Hinges (butt + parliament) are sold in PAIRS** — count 2 pc per leaf minimum in take-offs.
- Door bolts: match UP + DOWN + dust socket per flush-bolt door; length by door leaf height (8–24 in / 203–610 mm).
- Specify by **code + finish-code + size** (e.g. `L1037-SB-L`); confirm price/finish/stock with AELLA (prices glyph-decoded, VAT unstated).
