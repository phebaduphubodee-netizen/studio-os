## Turn 6 — surrogation / goal displacement: prevention structures

Goodhart's law + surrogation (the proxy replaces the construct). In creative
pipelines it manifests as the **Junior Trap / micro-iteration overload** (endless
polish of one asset, over-built sim rigs for background assets never seen close
up) — "believability beats realism". Six structures that PREVENT rather than
detect: (1) progressive-fidelity approvals + sequential locks (no polish before
blocking sign-off); (2) **capped pre-agreed revision round budgets** with
disposition logging per note (accepted/rejected/deferred); (3) Definition of
Done as an upfront checklist; (4) timeboxing + WIP limits; (5) **Stage-Gate
Go/Kill with independent gatekeepers** — formal governance kills "zombie
projects that survive on momentum"; (6) multi-metric outcome regularization (no
agent optimized against one unconstrained target).

## Turn 7 — blocked critical path: what the team does while waiting

Unanimous prescription: **escalate + swarm the blocker while HOLDING work in
progress** — WIP limits exist precisely to forbid bypassing a blocked item into
new lower-value work. Documented harms of filling the wait: context switching /
quality loss; masking the severity of the bottleneck ("the critical path
stagnates while the team is busy"); the Junior Trap; and **invalidated rework**
— if the external decision-maker later changes upstream assumptions, all
polishing done during the wait is invalidated at once.

## Turn 8 — hero vs background effort budgeting (LOD policy)

Effort is budgeted by camera framing and role in the shot: context-driven rig
density (over-building a background asset's sim rig is a NAMED costly failure
mode — it slows every iteration of the whole shot); when budget is tight,
degrade non-hero resolution FIRST; blocking approved before polish;
"supervisors prefer rough work heading the right direction over polished work
heading the wrong direction — an early rough review redirects in 5 minutes vs
2 wasted days"; "don't re-daily tiny changes" (micro-tweaks go to informal
internal rounds, dailies are for meaningful deltas); capped revision budgets;
**diagnose the root note** — "make it feel heavier" is a timing/anticipation
symptom, not a literal mass instruction; executing notes literally burns rounds
on the wrong layer of the shot.

## Turn 9 — converting "it doesn't look real" into bounded acceptance

Mature pipelines never treat subjective verdicts as literal engineering
instructions: (1) **pre-agreed references + styleframes BEFORE 3D execution** —
disputes are then settled by pulling up the approved reference, turning a vague
debate into a testable comparison; (2) translate feelings into diagnostic
symptoms (12-principles checklist; believability over realism); (3) capped
round budgets + disposition logging; (4) sequential locks with **formal reopen
penalties** — a note that contradicts an approved upstream stage is a formal
"reopen" carrying a recalculated cascading cost presented to the director,
forcing a Go/Kill on the revision itself; (5) A/B side-by-side playblasts to
resolve contested verdicts visually instead of in prose.
