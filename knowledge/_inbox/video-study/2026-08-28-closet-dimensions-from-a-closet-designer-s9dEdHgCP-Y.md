## 2. THE ORDERING RULE THAT CHANGES THE CASEWORK, NOT THE STYLING

`[04:09]` *"once you've figured out **where your hanging is going to go** — because that's
the most important thing — **you can put folded clothes elsewhere, you can't put hanging
clothes anywhere else but on a rod**, so you need to **accommodate that stuff first.**"*

Hanging is planned first and everything else fills in around it. Our nine cells are, in the
spec, interchangeable containers; a real design starts by allocating **double-hang vs
medium vs long-hang** and derives the shelf positions from that.

Her worked layout `[04:44]`: a **double-hang** section on one side, a **long-hang** section
on the other, **shelves and drawers between them**, shoes and bags on the top shelf.

### 2.1 CORNERS: HANGING BEATS SHELVING
`[05:56]` *"the thing you want to be most mindful of is **getting access to the corners**…
the better access is **hanging**, because you can always take a hanger and **slide it
over**. If you have shelves into this corner it's much more difficult to get in there and
see past this — so when I was a closet designer we would like to do **hanging on either
side**."*

### 2.2 RETURN WALLS EAT VISIBLE DEPTH
`[02:57]` *"with the bifold doors… the **stack back** of the doors open, I'm losing about
**7 inches** of visual access to that back corner, plus the **return wall**, which is just
about **8 inches** or so on each side — so right there **I can't see about 17 inches** into
that closet."*

**≈ 178 mm + 203 mm = 432 mm of closet you cannot see.** For our OPEN-front wardrobe there
are no doors, so this is the argument FOR the open front — and it is also the reason the
contents are fully exposed and therefore fully judged.

### 2.3 WHY AN OPEN / VISIBLE CLOSET EXISTS AT ALL
`[01:11]` *"if you can't see it you don't know you own it — so you go to look for that black
purse, you can't find it, you go buy another one, and the minute you do you find that black
purse. **Wall closets give you the view of everything all at once**, if you do the doors
right."*

A design rationale for the open front, which our room has and has never justified in the
spec (R10: every object must justify its existence — including the *absence* of doors).

## 3. THE FOLD MODULE — a folded stack has a derivable footprint

`icCL89J6w54` is a garment maker folding for retail display, and the method is geometric:

`[02:15]`-`[04:01]`, for a t-shirt:
1. *"you'll need a **flat surface**… lay your t-shirt **front side down**, make sure it's
   completely flat."*
2. *"fold the **bottom hemline** — we'll go about **a sixth or seventh of the way** — and
   fold it in… **this is done to shorten the overall length.**"*
3. *"use our **fifth lines**: if you look at your t-shirt as if it's spread into fifths —
   one, two, three, four, five — we'll **fold along the side of each fifth fold line**…
   making sure that both of these sections are **equal**."*
4. *"then you'll **fold it in half**."*

And the failure mode, named `[02:50]`: *"if they're not equal, what's going to happen is
you're going to get a **wonky t-shirt**, and any graphics you have on the front will appear
**off-centre**."* `[03:24]` *"make sure that both of your fold lines are **equidistant** —
the same distance and the same size."*

> **This gives the stack a MODULE.** Folded width ≈ **3/5 of the garment width**; folded
> length ≈ **(garment length − 1/6) / 2**. Every piece folded this way has the *same*
> footprint, which is what makes a stack read as a stack: square edges, a flat face, and a
> repeating value step down the pile. Our shelf items have no stated fold rule, so their
> footprint is whatever the mesh happened to be.
>
> And the reason it matters is stated `[00:34]`: *"**how you present your clothes on your
> racks and on your shelves is going to be an indication of how your brand is going to
> resonate**… a good presented store will go a long way towards pushing this exclusive and
> premium look."* The stack is read as a signal of care — which is exactly the register in
> which the owner's *"ไม่เป็นธรรมชาติ"* verdict sits.

Note the same tension already recorded: this is **retail** folding. It gives the module and
the equidistant rule; it does not tell us how many stacks per cell or how full a *home*
shelf is (still VG-01).
