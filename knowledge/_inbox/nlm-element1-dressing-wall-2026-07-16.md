## GROUNDED — Ergonomics of a dressing/wardrobe wall (Ask 1)
Source: **Panero & Zelnik, *Human Dimension and Interior Space*** (`a1383994`); Postell (`01277b77`).
- **Makeup-mirror (seated):** mirror-centre / eye level **1120–1267 mm AFF**; face-to-mirror
  viewing distance **457–610 mm**; useful mirror field height **610–762 mm**.
- **Dressing vanity (seated):** surface **711–762 mm**; usable width **1067–1372 mm**;
  knee/thigh clearance under apron **≥ 196 mm**; seat popliteal **406–432 mm**.
- **Vanity sharing a wardrobe run:** the 914 mm closet-access clearance **is not enough** where
  a seated vanity + circulation coincide → widen to **1067–1168 mm**.
- **Hang-rail:** arm-swing aisle to select a garment **914 mm**; rod-to-shelf-above **+102 mm**
  (hanger lift-off). *(Hanger spacing/density: not in sources.)*
- **Folded-shelf reach:** comfortable front-to-back reach (5th-pct F) **457 mm** → shelves
  deeper than this force a stretch; a 600 mm carcass wants daily goods at the front.

## GROUNDED — Composition / escaping the mono-material trap (Ask 4)
Source: **Albers, *Interaction of Color*** (`28fa10cb`); **Postell, *Furniture Design*** (`109b32e1`);
Interior-Design-Knowledge-Structuring (`56678948`).
- **60-30-10, "quantity = size × recurrence" (Albers):** the same wood on the *floor* (60%) *and*
  a feature wall (30%) is what manufactures a mono-timber monopoly. **Escape:** demote the floor's
  visual weight (a large soft rug) so the wood reads as the **30% secondary**; keep metal as the
  **10% accent**. → extends [[color-composition]] 60-30-10 with a concrete de-monopoly move.
- **Offset a light-wood field (Albers "a ground subtracts its own hue"):** warm-painted walls
  *wash the wood out*; put the 60% in **matte, cool-toned off-white plaster / limewash / textured
  linen** — low-frequency texture rests the eye and lets the grain read.
- **Wood vs black-framed glass = intentional separation, not a clash (Albers "hard boundary =
  separation"):** a black-alu frame *frames* the wood; the extreme tonal contrast reads as a
  structural statement, not bleeding.
- **Many functions → few masses:** slats read as a calm continuous field ("stripes are overlooked
  as almost shapeless"); group N functional sub-elements into an **odd number of masses** (3) for a
  legible, un-busy wall (Odd Rule).

## CONVENTION — material behaviour in AC humid-tropical (Ask 3, OUTSIDE sources — verify)
- Veneer over **MR engineered core > solid** for stability; EMC target **~10–14 %** (Thailand);
  **acclimatise 72 h–1 wk with AC on** before fixing. *(Vault-corroborated direction: engineered >
  solid in tropics; edge-banding = the moisture seal — [[residential-materials]], [[millwork-casework]].)*
- **Film-forming finish (satin PU/lacquer) > open-pore oil** for veneer in humidity swings
  (slows moisture exchange → resists delamination); **edge-band every exposed edge**.
- **Open (doorless) wardrobe** airs garments (anti-mildew, good in the tropics) **but collects
  dust** → keep dust-sensitive items in closed drawers.
- **Satin/brushed brass** hides fingerprints + water-spots; **polished** shows every mark;
  lacquer prevents tarnish but chips blotchily. *(Vault: AELLA Satin Brass line — [[aella-hardware-th]].)*
