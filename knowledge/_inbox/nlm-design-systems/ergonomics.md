Here is the compilation of key residential interior clearances and 
anthropometric standards based on *Human Dimension and Interior Space* by 
Julius Panero and Martin Zelnik. 

As requested, all measurements are provided in **metric millimeters (mm)** with
imperial inches in parentheses.

### **1. Living Areas (Section 2.1)**
The clearances in living spaces must accommodate both static furniture 
arrangements and the dynamic movements of users (such as reaching, circulating,
and extending legs).

| Clearance / Anthropometric Standard | Metric (mm) | Imperial (in) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sofa to coffee table gap** (accommodates reach without rising) | **406 – 
457 mm** | (16 – 18 in) [1, 2] |
| **Conversation grouping distance** (maximum communications factor) | **2134 –
2845 mm** | (84 – 112 in) [1] |
| **Circulation past seating** (head-on access between table and chair) | **610
mm** | (24 in) [1, 2] |
| **TV viewing distance vs. screen size** | *Not specified in sources* | *Not 
specified* |

the horizontal sight line) and distances for workstation display consoles 
(457–559 mm / 18–22 in) [3, 4], they do not explicitly detail residential TV

### **2. Dining Spaces (Section 2.2)**
Dining space dimensions are governed by the size of the place setting, the 
maximum body breadth of the diners, and the clearance required to pull out 
chairs and circulate.

| Clearance / Anthropometric Standard | Metric (mm) | Imperial (in) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Per-person place setting width** (optimal, allows for elbow projection) | 
**762 mm** | (30 in) [5] |
| **Per-person place setting width** (minimal) | **610 mm** | (24 in) [5] |
| **Chair pull-back zone** (clearance for a person rising from the table) | 
**914 mm** | (36 in) [6] |
| **Table edge to wall/obstruction** (minimum, allowing chair access but *no* 
circulation behind) | **762 – 914 mm** | (30 – 36 in) [7] |
| **Table edge to wall/obstruction** (with 1-lane head-on circulation behind 
seated diner) | **1219 mm** | (48 in) [7, 8] |
| **Table edge to wall/obstruction** (with 2-lane service circulation behind 
extended chair) | **1524 mm** | (60 in) [9] |
