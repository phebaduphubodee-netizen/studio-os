### **1. Flooring**
When specifying flooring, fundamental material properties such as dimensional 
movement (expansion/contraction), absorptive quality, and volatile organic 
compound (VOC) emissions must be evaluated [1].

| Material Type | Typical Residential Applications | Durability & Maintenance* 
| Moisture & Heat Suitability (Tropical)* | Performance Metrics / Standards to 
Check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Solid Wood** | Living areas, bedrooms, dining | High durability but 
susceptible to scratches. Requires periodic refinishing and protective sealing.
| **Poor.** Highly susceptible to humidity and temperature fluctuations, 
leading to severe dimensional movement (warping/cupping) [1]. | Janka Hardness 
Scale*, VOC levels for finishes [1]. |
| **Engineered Wood** | Living areas, bedrooms, basements | Good durability. 
Real wood veneer over a stable core; easier to maintain than solid wood but has
limited refinishing potential. | **Moderate.** The cross-layered core provides 
better stability against humidity and heat than solid wood, but extreme 
moisture should be avoided. | Wear layer thickness*, VOC emissions for 
binders/adhesives [1]. |
| **Laminate** | Kitchens, living areas, playrooms | High scratch and dent 
resistance. Easy to sweep and damp-mop. Cannot be refinished. | **Moderate to 
Poor.** The top layer is heat resistant, but high humidity or standing water 
can cause the composite fiberboard core to irreversibly swell at the seams. | 
AC (Abrasion Class) Rating*. |
| **Tile (Ceramic/ Porcelain)** | Kitchens, bathrooms, entries, living areas | 
Extremely high durability. Resists scratches and stains. Grout lines require 
periodic cleaning and sealing. | **Excellent.** Highly resistant to moisture, 
mold, and heat. Naturally cool to the touch, making it ideal for tropical 
climates. | PEI (Porcelain Enamel Institute) Rating*, DCOF (Dynamic Coefficient
of Friction) for slip resistance*. |
| **Stone (Natural)** | Entryways, bathrooms, high-end living spaces | High 
durability but variable by stone. Requires specialized cleaners and regular 
sealing due to its natural absorptive quality [1]. | **Excellent.** Cool 
underfoot and withstands high heat. Must be properly sealed in humid climates 
to prevent mold in the pores. | Water absorption rate, Mohs hardness scale*. |
| **Vinyl (LVT/LVP)** | Bathrooms, kitchens, basements, utility rooms | Highly 
durable, resilient, and easy to maintain. Resists chipping and tearing. | 
**Excellent.** 100% waterproof and highly stable in humid environments. Direct,
intense tropical sunlight can sometimes cause fading or warping if not 
UV-protected. | Wear layer thickness (measured in mil)*, VOC emissions for 
indoor air quality [1]. |

### **2. Wall Finishes**
For wall finishes, specifiers must consider surface qualities, 
aging/weathering, and how the material impacts the acoustic performance of the 
room [1, 3].

| Material Type | Typical Residential Applications | Durability & Maintenance* 
| Moisture & Heat Suitability (Tropical)* | Performance Metrics / Standards to 
Check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Paint** | All interior walls and ceilings | Maintenance depends on sheen 
(flat is hard to clean; satin/semi-gloss is easily washable). Needs repainting 
over time. | **Good.** Formulations with anti-microbial and mildew-resistant 
additives are highly suitable for humid, tropical environments. | VOC levels 
[1], Light Reflectance Value (LRV)*. |
| **Wallcovering (Wallpaper)** | Dining rooms, bedrooms, accent walls | Paper 
is fragile and hard to clean. Vinyl wallcoverings are highly durable and 
scrubbable. | **Poor to Moderate.** In tropical climates, standard vinyl 
wallcoverings can trap moisture behind the wall, promoting hidden mold growth. 
Micro-vented options are required*. | Flame spread ratings*, ACT guidelines if 
using textile wallcoverings [4]. |
| **Wood Paneling** | Accent walls, libraries, living areas | Durable but 
requires dusting and occasional oiling/polishing. Scratches can be repaired. | 
**Poor.** Similar to wood floors, paneling will experience dimensional movement
(expansion) in high-humidity tropical climates [1]. | FSC Certification*, Sound
Transmission Class (STC) if used as a demountable partition [3]. |
| **Tile** | Bathroom walls, showers, kitchen backsplashes | Extremely durable 
and easy to wipe clean. Grout requires sealing and maintenance to prevent 
discoloration. | **Excellent.** Impervious to humidity, steam, and heat, making
it perfect for wet and tropical zones. | Moisture absorption rate*, Noise 
Reduction Coefficient (NRC) (tile reflects sound, lowering acoustic comfort) 
[3]. |

### **3. Countertops**
Countertops undergo rigorous use and must be evaluated for their compressive 
strength, density, and surface absorptive qualities [1]. 

| Material Type | Typical Residential Applications | Durability & Maintenance* 
| Moisture & Heat Suitability (Tropical)* | Performance Metrics / Standards to 
Check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Stone (Granite/ Marble)** | Kitchen islands, bathroom vanities | Very 
durable and heat-resistant. Marble scratches and etches easily. Both have high 
absorptive qualities and require routine sealing to prevent stains [1]. | 
**Excellent.** Highly resistant to ambient heat and moisture. | Density [1], 
Mohs hardness scale*. |
| **Engineered Quartz** | Kitchen perimeters, islands, vanities | Extremely 
durable, non-porous, and scratch-resistant. Zero maintenance (no sealing 
required). | **Excellent.** Impervious to humidity. Resin binders can be 
sensitive to direct, extreme heat (e.g., placing a hot pan directly on the 
surface), but ambient tropical heat is a non-issue. | NSF Certification (food 
safety)*, VOCs from resin binders [1]. |
| **Solid Surface (e.g., Corian)** | Bathrooms, seamless kitchen sinks | 
Durable and completely non-porous. Scratches easily but can be seamlessly 
sanded and repaired. | **Excellent.** Unaffected by tropical humidity and 
highly mold-resistant. Can warp under extreme, direct heat. | Heat resistance 
thresholds*, Greenguard certification*. |
| **Laminate** | Budget-friendly kitchens, laundry rooms, bathrooms | Good 
durability for the cost. Easy to clean. Susceptible to deep scratches and burns
which cannot be repaired. | **Moderate.** The melamine/plastic surface handles 
humidity well, but the particleboard substrate will swell irreversibly if 
tropical moisture penetrates the seams [1]. | NEMA LD-3 standards (wear/stain 
resistance)*. |
| **Wood (Butcher Block)** | Kitchen islands, prep stations | High maintenance.
Requires frequent oiling and conditioning. Shows knife marks and dents easily. 
| **Poor.** High absorptive quality causes it to harbor moisture, swell, and 
potentially rot or harbor bacteria in humid, tropical climates [1]. | 
FDA-approved food-safe sealants*, Dimensional movement metrics [1]. |

### **4. Textiles (Upholstery & Drapery)**
Textile selection is governed by strict industry performance guidelines to 
ensure fabrics can withstand physical stress and environmental factors [4].

| Material Type | Typical Residential Applications | Durability & Maintenance |
Moisture & Heat Suitability (Tropical)* | Performance Metrics / Standards to 
Check |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Upholstery** | Sofas, dining chairs, headboards | Durability is defined by 
abrasion resistance. Fabrics are tested by pulling them tight and rubbing them 
with a cotton duck/wire screen or worsted wool [5]. | **Moderate.** Natural 
fibers (cotton/linen) breathe well in heat but absorb moisture. Solution-dyed 
acrylics or synthetic blends resist tropical mildew and humidity better. | 
**Association for Contract Textiles (ACT) standards** [4]. **Wyzenbeek Test 
(ASTM D4157):** Minimum 15,000 double rubs (general) or 30,000+ (heavy duty) 
[5]. **Martindale Test (ISO 12947-2):** Minimum 20,000 cycles (general) or 
40,000+ (heavy duty) [5]. |
| **Drapery** | Window treatments, room dividers | Requires regular vacuuming 
and occasional dry cleaning. Must resist fading from UV exposure. | 
**Moderate.** Unlined natural fabrics can rot or grow mold in tropical 
humidity. Mildew-resistant synthetics and UV-protective linings are highly 
recommended to combat intense sun and moisture. | Colorfastness to light (UV 
resistance)*, Flammability/Flame spread ratings*, Dimensional stability 
(resistance to sagging) [1]. |
