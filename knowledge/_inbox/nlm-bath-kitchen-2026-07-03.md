**2. Wet/dry zoning**

- Dry zones (basin, WC, storage) protect cabinetry; GFCI receptacle within **914 mm** of each basin.

**3. Fixture ordering by frequency of use (Neufert) — matches designer GS-05**

- **Shower & tub** = least-frequent, longer use → **deepest point** (quiet zone, contains heat/humidity at the back walls).
