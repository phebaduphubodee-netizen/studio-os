## Status legend

- `[CONFIRMED]` — client approved in writing
- `[PENDING CLIENT]` — waiting client
- `[PENDING SUPPLIER]` — waiting supplier quote
- `[ESTIMATE]` — best-estimate, not validated
- `[CONFIRM]` — need data before estimating
- `[REJECTED]` — client declined
- `[MERGED]` — approved and merged to current BOQ
