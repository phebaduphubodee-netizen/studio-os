## 5. เทมเพลต Prompt มู้ดบอร์ด AI / AI moodboard prompt reference (thread `017_Prompt`)

Peat's reference prompt for generating a **high-end architectural material moodboard** with an AI image generator,
transcribed from `017_Prompt/files/457164_image.png` ("Interior Mood Board" reference sheet). Summary of the printed prompt:

- **Subject:** High-End Architectural Material Moodboard.
- **Composition:** an artistic, layered arrangement of physical material samples and tactile tiles; use a
  sophisticated overlapping technique.
- **Intent (author's note):** terminology polished so a high-end AI generator OR a design team understands the specific
  "luxury architectural" aesthetic being aimed for.
- **Worked example on the sheet** — a rendered moodboard plate titled *"RESIDENCE 102 — MATERIAL SCHEME: TACTILE LUXURY KITCHEN"*
  with labelled samples: Flooring = Pietra Grey Marble; Countertop = Silver Fox Marble; Cabinetry = Matte Taupe;
  Accent Glass = Fluted/Reeded with warm light; Accent Wood = Walnut; Metal Finishes = Aged Brass & Matte Black;
  Upholstery = Oatmeal Linen Blend.

Use as a template pattern (swap the material labels per project) for producing client-facing material moodboards; it is a
prompt-craft reference, not studio material-spec truth.
