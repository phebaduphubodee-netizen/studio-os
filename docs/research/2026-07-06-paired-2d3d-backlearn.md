# Paired 2D-plan / 3D corpora — backwards-learning research record

**Date:** 2026-07-06 · **Trigger:** owner — "การถอดแบบ 2D→3D ยังดีไม่พอ; ไปหางานจริงที่มีทั้ง 2D และ 3D ของงานเดียวกัน แล้วเรียนรู้ย้อนกลับ"
**Method:** 7-angle web sweep (academic datasets / reconstruction methods / VLM-era benchmarks / pro portfolios / Thai market / drafting-symbol standards / commercial 2D→3D tools) → adversarial verification of the top findings → synthesis against the pipeline's 5 semantic failure classes.
**Stats:** 114 findings → 112 unique → 16 adversarially verified (0 refuted); 63 raw lessons. Raw findings + verdicts: `2026-07-06-paired-2d3d-backlearn-raw.json`.
**Failure classes referenced throughout:** F1 furniture identity · F2 facing/orientation · F3 indoor-vs-outdoor · F4 thin-line features (sliding glass / glass facade) · F5 floor membership (ground-level elements drawn in an upper-floor sheet).
**Verification tiers:** VERIFIED = fact-checker fetched the primary page; *(partial)* = verified with corrections (noted inline); UNVERIFIED = single-agent claim, re-check before any rule/gate cites it. Statutory values never come from this record — `knowledge/codes-th/` outranks.

## 2. Failure-class map

**F1 — furniture identity (desk↔cabinet; label-cluster↔furniture).**
Sources: FloorPlanCAD per-line furniture classes (https://floorplancad.github.io/) is the direct benchmark; CubiCasa5K 11-icon set (https://zenodo.org/record/2613548) for marketing-dialect plans; Structured3D furnished-vs-empty renders of the same rooms answer "what did this symbol denote" with zero human labels *(partial: full/simple/empty is panorama-only)*; LIFULL/Rent3D++ photos verify symbols on real listings. Method insights (unverified tier): SymPoint's per-class PQ table (https://github.com/nicehuster/SymPoint) shows even SOTA on clean vectors scores **0.0 PQ on revolving/rolling doors, 6.8 on bay windows** vs 83.3 overall — long-tail symbols have a published machine ceiling; ArchPlanVQA (https://ascelibrary.org/doi/10.1061/JCCEE5.CPENG-7571) puts general VLMs at 33–38% on CAD semantics; AECV-Bench (https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.04819) shows VLMs at ~0.95 on OCR but 0.40–0.55 on door/window counting; DeepFloorplan's own reported failure — an arrow icon read as wall — is our dimension-label-cluster error's published twin; Archtoolbox (https://www.archtoolbox.com/architectural-floor-plan-symbols/) catalogs the annotation-symbol family (door tags, glazing tags, room IDs, grid callouts) that gets misread as furniture; R2V's lesson: identity is repaired by a **deterministic global-consistency solver (integer programming)**, not a better classifier.
**Most actionable lesson:** route by symbol-class confidence — a trained/template symbol-spotter over vector primitives (FloorPlanCAD taxonomy) handles the head; long-tail + annotation-cluster classes go to the owner ledger; never a general VLM (three independent benchmarks agree, all unverified individually but convergent).

**F2 — facing/orientation.**
Sources: 3D-FRONT per-instance rotation *(partial — quaternion field unconfirmed on-page)* is the only at-scale facing GT; Structured3D bbox basis matrices; CubiCasa5K swing arcs; DEDE's 24-orientation variants (UNVERIFIED) as a perturbation set. Symbol geometry rules from Cedreo/PlanSnapper (UNVERIFIED): bed headboard strip = head end, sofa backrest strip = back, armchair open side = front, desk chair-dot = sit side, TV face = thin line away from wall. Negative evidence: 3DSRBench (https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.07825, UNVERIFIED) — VLMs under 50% on left/right orientation; SceneCritic (https://arxiv.org/html/2604.13035v1, UNVERIFIED) — VLM judging of renders is viewpoint-unstable (75%↔55% same scene) while **symbolic orientation checks hit 83–94% human agreement**; FloorplanQA (UNVERIFIED) — even with perfect structured input, LLM spatial reasoning fails.
**Most actionable lesson:** facing must stay deterministic-symbolic (asymmetry-of-the-symbol rules + owner-signed rot ledger); VLMs are measurably blind here — this independently vindicates the existing `confirmed_rot` ledger design.

**F3 — indoor vs outdoor zoning.**
Sources: Rent3D++ (VERIFIED) — explicit {outdoor, balcony, terrace} labels on real plans, the only curated real F3 benchmark; CubiCasa5K "Outdoor" + "Railing" classes; Swiss Dwellings balcony/loggia areas + railing separators; ZInD raw/complete/visible triplet formalizes "what the sensor sees vs what the space is"; Structured3D balcony/garden room types (no explicit flag). Industry field data: CubiCasa's Capterra complaint "a random exterior fence being included in GLA floorspace" *(UNVERIFIED, truncated in source data)* = F3 shipping in production.
**Most actionable lesson:** F3 is a labeled-classification problem only at the room-polygon level (Rent3D++/CubiCasa taxonomies), and the boundary evidence is the railing/glazing line class — the machine can *flag* candidates (area beyond a railing/GLAZ line) but the indoor/outdoor call stays owner-signed, as our lounge-vs-terrace incident already proved.

**F4 — thin-line features (sliding glass, glass facades).**
Sources: FloorPlanCAD is the **only** large dataset typing sliding doors and naming **curtain wall** as a class — and every paper's table shows curtain wall/railing among the worst classes (~20–35 PQ per SymPoint, UNVERIFIED) even on clean vectors; ASA 2554 (UNVERIFIED) makes it normative for Thailand: glass lives on **A-GLAZ-FULL at pen 0.25 — deliberately thinner than walls**, mirrored by AIA/NCS A-GLAZ-* family (UNVERIFIED, https://facilities.duke.edu/sites/default/files/AIA%20CAD%20Layer%20Guidelines.pdf); TOSTEM Thailand (UNVERIFIED) gives the deterministic Thai rule: arrow position = sliding direction, no-arrow panel = fixed glass; ZInD's W/D/O "opening" class + its own docs warning of cross-room W/D/O misalignment; R2V/DeepFloorplan lineage proves glass is *representationally absent* from thick-stroke methods; Ching (UNVERIFIED) explains why: heavy lineweight profiles *cut* elements — glass isn't cut mass, so it reads thin by construction.
**Most actionable lesson:** thin ≠ noise — glass is thin **by standard** (ASA layer pen widths); run a second thin-stroke extraction pass that promotes thin lines *spanning wall gaps or closing room-boundary loops* to glazing candidates (our manual-glass re-injection is the right shape; the promotion rule can now cite a standard). *(Rests on unverified ASA/TOSTEM pages — verify the two PDFs/articles before codifying.)*

**F5 — floor membership (ground-level elements in an upper-floor plan).**
Sources: ZInD multi-floor homes (panos show trees through glass that plans omit = natural negatives); Swiss Dwellings floor_id; Structured3D weak. Convention layer (all UNVERIFIED): ISO 7519:2025 preview — above-ground projections drawn long-dash-dotted wide (type 04.2), below-ground dashed wide (02.2); ASA 2554 p.8-8 — objects above/below the cut plane use thin dashed lines; AIA/NCS — a tree at grade is discipline **L** (L-PLNT-TREE) vs a planter on structure **A-FURN-PLNT**; Draftscapes — tree symbols drawn at *mature canopy diameter* routinely overlap slabs they don't stand on; trunk dot = true ground position; Ching — dashed = above cut plane.
**Most actionable lesson:** floor membership is encoded in **linetype + layer discipline, not position** — dashed/thin-dashed linework and canopy-circle-overlapping-slab must be demoted to "not this floor" candidates and surfaced to the owner, never auto-placed (exactly the garden-trees-below miss). *(Entire convention basis is unverified-tier; the two standards PDFs are the verification priority.)*

## 3. Backwards-learning benchmark design

**Reader output under test (given):** per element `{id, kind, position(mm), rotation(deg), zone, indoor|outdoor}` + per plan `{floor_id}` per element.

**Ground-truth minting per source:**
- *Synthetic tier (labels free):* Structured3D `annotation_3d.json` + `bbox_3d.json` → project to vector plan via the paper's own floor-projection recipe; 3D-FRONT scene JSON → render plan-style top-down views with known per-instance pose. GT = the 3D annotation itself. Confirm the 3D-FRONT rotation field on first download *(partial verification)*.
- *Vector-semantics tier:* FloorPlanCAD SVG (semantic_id, instance_id) per primitive → GT for F1/F4 identity at primitive level; CubiCasa5K SVG polygons + swing arcs → F1/F2/F3.
- *Real-photo tier (human-adjudicated once, then frozen):* Rent3D++ room categories (F3 GT as shipped); BMA/DPT Thai pairs + thinkofliving photos → one-time annotation pass producing a frozen `gt.json` per plan (annotator ≠ owner; any junior can adjudicate against a photo — that is the point of backwards learning).

**Per-failure-class metrics:**
- **F1 identity:** per-kind precision/recall/F1 over a fixed taxonomy mapped onto FloorPlanCAD's 35 classes + a Thai extension; report **per-class**, never only macro (SymPoint's 0.0-PQ tail classes vanish inside a macro average). Separate confusion sub-metric for annotation-cluster-vs-furniture (binary: is-this-ink-an-object), since that's our historical failure.
- **F2 facing:** angular error buckets — exact (≤5°), cardinal-correct (≤45°), flipped (180°±45°), wrong; score = % cardinal-correct on facing-asymmetric kinds only (bed/sofa/chair/TV/door-swing); symmetric kinds excluded a priori. Evaluate rotation-augmented (rotate plan 90/180/270 + DEDE's 24-orientation set when verified) — reader must be equivariant.
- **F3 indoor/outdoor:** per-zone binary accuracy + boundary-evidence recall (did the reader emit a glazing/railing boundary where GT has one). GT = Rent3D++ labels / photo adjudication.
- **F4 thin-line features:** detection P/R of openings with subtype accuracy {hinged, sliding, fixed, opening/no-door} against FloorPlanCAD subtypes + Thai permit-set door/window schedules (ป./น. tags cross-referenced — the schedule inside the same PDF is machine-readable GT, per dsignsomething, UNVERIFIED); plus a **room-closure test**: % of GT room boundaries fully closed once glazing candidates are included (our sliding-glass-facade failure expressed as a metric).
- **F5 floor membership:** per-element binary this-floor/not-this-floor F1, GT from multi-storey pairs (which sheet carries the element + perspective/photo confirmation). Score dashed-line and canopy-overlap demotion rules separately.

**Matching rule:** predicted↔GT element matched by kind-agnostic footprint IoU ≥0.5 (geometry is solved, so matching is nearly bijective); all metrics computed on matched pairs → semantic scores are not polluted by detection misses (reported separately as completeness — which our gate already owns).

**Sample sizes (binomial: to see a 10-pp accuracy difference at ~80% power you need ≈150–200 judged items per cell):**
- F1: ~200 instances/major class → ~2,000 instances ≈ 60–100 furnished plans (Structured3D/3D-FRONT free; ~30 Thai plans hand-adjudicated for the transfer check).
- F2: ~300 facing-asymmetric instances (3D-FRONT gives this in <50 rooms; ~100 real-photo confirmations for the Thai dialect).
- F3: ~150 outdoor-candidate zones — needs real data: all 215 Rent3D++ + ~50 Thai balcony/terrace cases.
- F4: ~200 openings with subtype GT (FloorPlanCAD slice + all openings in the 7 DPT permit sets + ~20 condo plans).
- F5: ~100 multi-storey element cases (BMA 2-storey designs ≈ 1–3 cases each → need most of the ~100-design set + ZInD multi-floor homes).

**Harness:** `benchmark_reader.py` consumes `(plan.pdf|svg, gt.json)` pairs, emits per-class scorecard; wire as a CI-style regression gate so any pipeline change re-scores — the owner-free replacement for "owner eyeballs every rebuild". Track the **owner-touch metric**: number of ledger signatures needed to reach 100% per class — the product KPI (corrections cheap), directly measurable on this benchmark.

## 5. What the industry makes humans fix

All entries in this section are UNVERIFIED-tier (tool pages + review sites, several bot-blocked); the *convergence* across five independent vendors is the finding.

- **RoomSketcher AI Convert** (https://www.roomsketcher.com/features/ai-convert/): AI maps ONLY "walls, doors, and windows"; furniture conversion — "Not yet, but stay tuned!" despite training on 50,000+ real plans. Furniture identity (F1) is explicitly unshipped; humans add everything semantic post-conversion.
- **CubiCasa** (https://help.cubi.casa/en/articles/7913335-change-requests): the change-request taxonomy is the industry's admission list — window/door TYPES, room labels, fixed-furniture add/remove = exactly F1+F4. Field complaint: an exterior fence counted into indoor GLA (F3/F5 in production), corrections "taking half a dozen emails", and no source-evidence overlay for the verifier — the precise gap our `raster_overlay` pre-owner gate closes.
- **Planner 5D** (https://planner5d.com/ai): closed furniture set ("beds, bathrooms, tables… might not identify a refrigerator"), 10-min-to-24-h latency = human-in-the-loop queue signature; low ratings trigger "a second review" (human correction loop).
- **getfloorplan.com** (https://getfloorplan.com/): "a Quality Assurance team checks every AI floor plan" — a pure-play AI vendor ships **100% human-verified** output at ~฿1,200/plan; humans adjudicate F1/F2/F3 on every order.
- **HomeByMe drawing service** (https://home.by.me/en/services/floorplan-drawing-service/): even professional human redrawers ask the customer to supply "room names and window types" as metadata. **This is the strongest single datum:** F1/F4 are not machine-vision gaps but *information-theoretic gaps in the drawing itself* — the drawing does not contain the answer, so someone who knows the design must supply it as structured data.
- **Foyr Neo** (https://foyr.com/learn/foyr-neo-2d-to-3d/): pro tool chose human tracing with machine assist over auto-read — human supplies semantics, machine supplies precision.
- **Floorplanner DXF export** (https://floorplanner.com/pricing, reviews): text exports as "a mass of little lines", furniture not exported — our vector-outlined-label problem is endemic to geometry-only interchange; identity does not survive the format.

**Implication for the owner-signature ledger:** the market's equilibrium, reached independently by every vendor, is *machine geometry + human semantics* — no one ships zero-touch semantic reading, and the best-funded players route F1/F3/F4/F5 through paid human QA on every unit. Our ledger is the same economics with two upgrades the market lacks: (1) corrections **persist** as owner-signed structured data (vendors re-fix per order; CubiCasa users burn six emails per correction), and (2) the machine **presents evidence** (overlay + candidate flags) instead of making the human hunt. HomeByMe's intake form is external proof that owner-supplied structured semantics is the correct interface, not a workaround. The ledger is not a stopgap until better AI — it is the industry's converged architecture, done with provenance.
