CONTEXT: The lumen/CCT half of residential bedroom lighting is already answered in
our knowledge base at reference tier (roughly 108–215 lux general, 323–538 lux
bedside, 2700–3000 K, accent:ambient about 3:1, Kelly's four layers including
cove). What is missing is PHOTOGRAPHER AND RENDER PRACTICE — how a sellable
bedroom photograph is actually lit — plus second sourcing for values our own files
flag as resting on a single source.

QUESTIONS:

1. For a hero interior photograph of a bedroom, which lighting layers do
professional interior photographers switch ON, OFF or dim — ceiling downlights,
cove/coffer, bedside lamps, wall sconces, decorative pendants — and what is the
stated reason for each choice? Cite named photographers, published workshops, or
trade-body guidance rather than generic blog advice.

2. What exposure approach dominates published interior photography — single
exposure, HDR bracketing, or "flambient" (flash + ambient blending) — and what

3. What lumens-per-metre bands do lighting manufacturers publish for cove and
coffer LED strip in a residential bedroom, and what fascia geometry (setback,
fascia height, distance to ceiling) do they specify for a given gradient length on
the ceiling? Name the manufacturers and give the numbers.

4. In marketing and listing photography of bedrooms, which lighting layer visually
dominates the final image, and under what conditions do professionals omit ceiling
downlights from the shot altogether? Any published analysis or style guides from
real-estate marketing bodies.

5. Corroborate or amend, each with an independent second source: (a) accent-to-
ambient ratio 3:1 for residential accent lighting; (b) a 20:1 maximum luminance

(d) 323–538 lux bedside reading; (e) CRI ≥ 90 for residential. For each, say
whether the second source agrees, disagrees, or does not address it.

6. What lumen output and shade luminance do bedside table lamps typically have in
practice, and what mounting-height band do manufacturers and designers specify for
bedside wall sconces relative to mattress top and to floor? Sourced millimetre

7. Where does a cove sit in Kelly's ambient/focal/decorative classification, and
