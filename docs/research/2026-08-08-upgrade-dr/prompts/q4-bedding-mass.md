CONTEXT: A 3D bedroom scene needs bedding built to measurable physical numbers
rather than eyeballed — both for cloth simulation parameters and for sizing bought
assets. The cloth solver's fabric table in use today is self-described as "retuned
Blender defaults", i.e. an untraced prior. Every number below needs a source.

QUESTIONS:

1. What published mappings exist from duvet tog rating to fill weight (g/m²) and to
settled loft thickness in mm, per fill class (down, feather, microfibre, wool),
across the common 4.5 / 10.5 / 13.5 tog bands?

2. How does down FILL POWER translate to loft height and areal density — what crest
loft in mm does an all-season double/queen duvet typically present, and how much
does that loft compress at the hem roll and under a resting hand?

3. When a duvet is folded at the foot of a bed (half-fold, third-fold, turned-back
fold), what stack heights result as a multiple of single-layer loft — how much does
self-weight compress the stack below the naive sum of layers?

4. What drop/overhang conventions do bedding manufacturers and styling guides use
at bed edges — comforter vs coverlet vs bedspread drop bands in mm, relative to
mattress thickness bands of roughly 200–350 mm?

5. What indentation depth and slump behaviour do the major pillow fill classes show
under a head-weight load of roughly 3–5 kg, and at what lean-back angle do styled
pillows settle when propped against a vertical support?

6. What are published areal weights (g/m²) and bending rigidity / Cusick drape
coefficient values for common bedding fabrics (percale, sateen, linen, brushed
cotton), and what accepted procedure maps these onto a cloth solver's mass and
bending-stiffness parameters? Name the test standards (e.g. BS 5058, ASTM D1388)
and give the mapping's source.

7. How do production cloth-simulation practitioners represent a quilted,
batting-filled duvet — loft via pressure or internal springs, shell thickness,
quilting-seam constraints, edge-roll formation — and what parameter bands do they
publish? Blender-specific answers welcome but name the version.

8. What turned-back fold depth and visible-sheet-reveal proportions do hotel and
property-staging bedding standards specify for a made bed? Give millimetre bands
with sources.
