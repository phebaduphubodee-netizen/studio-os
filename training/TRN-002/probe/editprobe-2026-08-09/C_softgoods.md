This is a 3D render of a bedroom interior, produced from measured geometry.

Add ONE new item and nothing else: a crumpled linen throw blanket draped over the
foot of the bed, with realistic folds, weight and soft shadow where it meets the
bedding.

Hard constraints — everything already in the frame is a specification:
- Do NOT move, rotate, resize, add or remove ANY existing object.
- Every existing silhouette, edge and contact point must land on exactly the same
  pixels. Only the throw is new, and it may occlude the bedding it rests on.
- The camera, framing and perspective are fixed.
- Do not restyle the room, do not change the lighting.
