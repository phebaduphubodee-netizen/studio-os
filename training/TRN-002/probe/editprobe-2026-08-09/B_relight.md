This is a 3D render of a bedroom interior, produced from measured geometry.

Relight the SAME scene for late afternoon: warm low sun entering from the window
side, longer shadows, and the interior lamps reading as switched on.

Hard constraints — the geometry is the specification, not a suggestion:
- Do NOT move, rotate, resize, add or remove any object.
- Every silhouette, edge and contact point must land on exactly the same pixels.
- The camera, framing and perspective are fixed.
Change only the lighting and what it does to the existing surfaces.
