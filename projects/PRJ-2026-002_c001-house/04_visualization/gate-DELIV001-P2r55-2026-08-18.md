## The mechanism, named before the knob
The lane already carried three decorrelation rungs (per-OBJECT random offset,
book-match ping-pong, per-leaf tone) and the repeat survived all three. The
p2r55 diagnosis is a RESONANCE, not a missing seed: the shader's "leaf" was
the TEXTURE TILE (1.19 m), the carcass boards sit ~0.6 m apart = 0.504 tile,
and a half-tile step into a ping-pong is a mirror — adjacent boards sampled
near-perfect reflections of each other. No offset seed can fix a grid whose
pitch is wrong.

## What was built
The across-grain leaf grid becomes the PHYSICAL one: `PLANK_PITCH_MM` (180 mm
— the real veneer leaf width this file already declares as law). Each 180 mm
leaf samples its own white-noise-chosen window of the flitch — continuous
inside the leaf, discontinuous at the joint (a slip-match lay-up), window
clamped to [0, 1-leaf] so no leaf crosses the texture border mid-leaf. The
per-leaf tone (±4%) re-keys onto the same physical grid. ALONG grain the
ping-pong stays untouched (the fail-soft top fold); a per-leaf Z slip was
considered and REFUSED — panels span their whole grain run, so any slip folds
the grain mid-panel, the exact p2r14 defect the mapping note forbids.
Known boundary, said plainly: horizontal faces (shelf tops) now leaf on BOTH
plan axes — a 180 mm mosaic rather than strips. Tops are minor in this frame;
if a critic files it, the fix is orientation-aware leafing, a new small item.
