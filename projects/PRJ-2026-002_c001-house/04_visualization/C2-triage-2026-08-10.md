## What the triage found that no single item says

**Nine of the twenty land on rows that have NO INSTRUMENT.** #3, #4, #12, #17, #19 map
onto DEBT-02, DEBT-14, DEBT-15, DEBT-16 — four of the nine debt rows this repo already
admits it cannot see. A blind human eye found in one pass what our instrument set is
structurally unable to report.

**And one item has no rule at all, in either direction.** #9 counts to zero: no sockets,
no switches, no handles, no vents, no reading lights, no skirting. R10 asks whether a
present object justifies itself. `deliverable_check` scores what is in the frame.
`placement_check` judges where built things sit. **Nothing anywhere asks what a room of
this type MUST contain and does not.** That is not a missing threshold, it is a missing
question — the same shape as R10 itself when the owner named it ("คุณไม่เคยตั้งคำถาม
ของแต่ละชิ้นว่ามันอยู่ตรงนี้สมเหตุสมผลมั้ย"), one step further out.

**#10 is the sharpest single finding.** A nightstand 80 mm below the mattress passes an
ergonomic band that only knows absolutes. The band is not wrong; it is answering a
different question from the one a person asks when they reach for a glass of water in
the dark.
