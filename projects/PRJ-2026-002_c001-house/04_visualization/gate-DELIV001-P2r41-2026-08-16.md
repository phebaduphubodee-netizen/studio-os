## 1. `cloth_edge` WAS MEASURING ONE MATERIAL AND CALLING IT A BOUNDARY

The rung asks, in its own words, *"where one bed cloth lies on another, does the
boundary CAST A LINE (finite thickness) instead of reading as a tonal ramp"*. Its
site was four typed fractions, frozen 2026-08-15 from the p2r34 frame.

**Decoded from the render's own matmask, that box is 99.6 % `bed_throw` and 0.4 %
`bed_duvet`** — on p2r34, its own declaration frame, and on p2r36 and p2r39. There is
no cloth-on-cloth boundary inside it. It scored 2.8 %, then 9.3 %, then 13.6 % of
columns against a control firing at 89–94 %, printed **READS AS PAINT** each time, and
**the p2r39 gate cited that number as the measurement confirming C2 §5.**

The rung already carried the guard this repo built after R11 — a positive control, so
a low score cannot mean "could not see". **The control proves the METHOD can see an
edge. Nothing ever checked that the BOX HELD ONE.**

**So the site is named, not typed** (`edge_shadow.contact_columns` +
`shadow_line_at`): give the rung two material names and it finds every column where
one lies directly on the other, in the frame's own mask, and measures there. Fewer
columns than it needs is `NoFeature` — could-not-look, never a clean absence. A box is
frozen so nobody can re-aim it at a flattering spot; **a rule that names the contact
cannot be re-aimed at all**, which is R9's own distinction between a typed coordinate
and a declared relationship, one level up.

### What the correct site measures — same frame, same light, same code

| contact | dips | median | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| `bed_throw` over `bed_coverlet` (the throw's hanging edge) | **98.8 %** | 28.1 codes | 335 |
| `bed_coverlet` over `bed_throw` (its other hanging edge) | **95.5 %** | 28.4 | 110 |
| `bed_pillow` over `bed_duvet` — CONTROL, the acquired sham hem | **88.7 %** | 23.0 | 415 |
| **`bed_duvet` over `bed_throw` — the throw's hem LYING FLAT** | **36.0 %** | **2.7** | 633 |

**The defect is real and it is one hem, not our cloth.** The same object, in the same
frame, under the same lights, lines 95–99 % of its hanging edges and 36 % of the one
edge that lies flat on another cloth. That is an argument no absolute number could
make, and the retired box could not have produced it. **0.406× the control, not
0.151× — WEAK, not paint.** The cut (≥ 0.5) is still broken, so P2's exit is unchanged
by this correction; what changes is that the number now points at a site.

Cross-checked against the resampler before publishing: measured at native 2400 px the
same contacts read 39.0 % / 88.8 %, so the LE1600 normalise is not manufacturing the
undershoot.

## 2. THE BED-CLOTH PLAN SLOT WAS SMALLER THAN THE MATTRESS IT HAD TO COVER

`_place_bed_cloth` scaled every candidate by `min(slot / native)` where the slot was
`min(bed outer line, mattress + 40 mm)`. On this bed:

    mattress the cover must cover     1820 x 1969 mm
    slot the cover was scaled into    1800 x 1949 mm

**The ceiling sat below the floor.** A duvet authored at 2184 × 2331 mm — 180 mm of
drape down each flank, which is what makes cloth read as cloth — was scaled to 0.824
until its hem sat inside the mattress edge. That is precisely the "flatter than the
solver's duvet" the eye reported at p2r31, and precisely why the one metric that round
ranked on (coverage) preferred the smallest candidate: **a cloth that cannot reach
past the mattress covers its plan most efficiently.**

It also explains the asset that lane actually bought. `ub806591a` was the only
candidate that reached a passing coverage AND two draping flanks under the old rule —
and it did so at **scale 1.196**, i.e. stretched past the ceiling the assertion law
exists to enforce. **The rule made stretching the only route to a candidate that
drapes.**

**The rule is now a CEILING and a FLOOR, never a target** (`bedcloth_rules.plan_scale`):

- TARGET — the asset as authored. `max_scale 1.0`: a mesh is never stretched.
- CEILING — the mattress plus the cover's own drop on each side (2612 × 2761 × 716 mm
  here), because cloth that hangs cannot reach further out in plan than it hangs down.
  Derived from the bed, so re-drawing the bed re-aims it (R9).
- FLOOR — it must actually cover the mattress: the spec's own
  `model_requirements.covers`, now applied by the same code that scales.

## 3. AND THE AUDITION HAD BEEN JUDGING A DIFFERENT OBJECT THAN THE BUILD RENDERS

`bedcloth_bench` and `bedcloth_shoot` each carried their own copy of the staging
rules, and the copy was the **pre-p2r32** one: parts under 33 % of the mattress plan
were deleted before anything was measured or photographed. p2r32 had already found
that this deletes real cloth — one set's 374 × 1600 mm turned-down runner among it —
and corrected the BUILD. **The two tools that choose what the build consumes were
never told.** For nine rounds the build dressed this bed with runners while the
audition judged the same files with their runners removed. (`8635b5b9` keeps **1 field
+ 6 accessory parts**; the old audition kept the field alone.) The bench also computed
a rotation and never applied it.

R9b's law about objects, applied to code: **a rule spread across the callers is a rule
with one exemption per caller.** Staging is now one module — `bedcloth_fit` (Blender
glue) over `bedcloth_rules` (pure, tested) — called by the build, the bench and the
shoot. What stays in the build is its POLICY, which an audition must not be able to
change: the sidecar door, the `model_fit` plan rung, the coverage cut, the value-ladder
re-dress and the shading normaliser.

**The rank is no longer coverage.** Three hard filters (never stretched · covers ≥ 80 %
by ray · falls past ≥ 2 flanks), then the machine stops and the eye judges style —
which is the owner's practitioner rung made into a tool. `relief_mm` is deliberately
NOT a filter: both of its failure directions are real and nothing in this repo can
ground a band between them, so a threshold there would be taste in disguise.

## 4. THE CLAUSE THAT HAD BLOCKED THE BEST CANDIDATE FOR THREE ROUNDS

p2r31 measured `8635b5b9` as the most convincing cloth of everything auditioned and
did not take it, because *"the signed DD says the room is made, not slept in"*. p2r39
repeated that and escalated the bedding item to the owner for the third round running.

**The clause exists — and it is a sentence about the PILLOWS.** In the canonical
spec's own words, the subject is named and then the prohibition follows:

> *"the bed head is a THREE-HEIGHT ladder … two upright euro shams … two plump
> sleeping pillows … and ONE greige-oatmeal terry lumbar cushion off-centre … Keep all
> three heights, keep the value steps between them, keep the asymmetry; do NOT level
> them into a matched pair, do NOT wash the shams up to the pillows, and **do NOT
> crease, dent or rumple any of them (the room is made, not slept in)**"*

The same spec says this about the cloth that lies on the bed, three sentences earlier:

> *"the bed's coverlet is SIMULATED CLOTH, not a slab … it lies on the mattress,
> **overhangs three sides and FALLS**, so its folds, the roll at the mattress edge and
> its never-level hem are all solved rather than drawn … **Keep the folds, keep the
> uneven hem** … do NOT iron the fall into a flat skirt or a box"*

**The signed spec asks for the folds two critics keep asking for, and forbids only the
dented pillow.** The clause was read as governing the bedding as a whole; it governs
the three head cushions, which is why `bed_pillow` is the one piece the measurement
found sitting at the LOWEST fold energy — that is the spec being obeyed, not a defect.

Same shape as the 2026-08-16 strategy entry: each item's WRITTEN REASON for existing
turned out to be wrong. Filed as a correction to my own p2r39 triage, not as a defect
in it.

**And the spec supplies a better number than the one I declared.** It says the cover
overhangs **three** sides. The chosen candidate falls past **two**. The bench's cut of
2 is looser than the spec, so that shortfall is declared here rather than hidden by
the threshold that let it through.

   the shoot. Reverse: restore `slot_w, slot_d = min(lw, rw+0.04), min(ld, rd+0.04)`
