## 3. AN INSTRUMENT THAT HAD BEEN BLIND FOR FIVE ROUNDS NOW SEES — AND FLIPS

`p2_exit.wood_boards` searched lags up to `hi = W // 2`, which **excludes lag = W/2** — the
lag of a TWO-tile repeat, i.e. exactly the case its own docstring names ("two adjacent
boards"). It printed `no peak above floor` every round while unable to see its headline
case, and C3 filed the tiling item against that clean **six times**.

Bound extended to `int(W * 0.75)`. This is not a loosened threshold: `ac` is already divided
by `(W - lag)`, so every lag is normalised by its own overlap, and the surrogates walk the
same path so the floor rises with the search. Two new controls pin it (`test_p2_exit`):
a 2-tile repeat must be caught (it scored **peak 0.000** before), and widening must not
make noise periodic.

**Live verdict flipped to PERIODIC** — peak 0.586 @ lag 199 px vs floor 0.577.

The margin is thin (1.6%), so the evidence is not the margin — it is **reproducibility**:

| frame | peak | lag | floor |
|---|---|---|---|
| p2r30 | 0.585 | 199 px | 0.582 |
| p2r34 | 0.586 | 199 px | 0.578 |
| p2r35 | 0.586 | 199 px | 0.577 |

Three frames rendered rounds apart under different light and different materials return the
same peak to three decimals at the same lag while the floor wobbles. **Noise does not do
that.** The eye agrees on the crop: the two carcass side panels carry the same figure.

**C3's tiling item is therefore ACCEPTED, not refuted — the first time our own instrument
has agreed with a critic on this class instead of contradicting it.** D-056 updated.
