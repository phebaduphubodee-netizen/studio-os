## What was corrected (mechanism, not numbers)
* `wholebed_rules.plan_overlap_frac` + `FIELD_MIN_OVERLAP 0.5` — a made-bed
  layer puts ≥ half its plan over the frame ("a part of the bed lies on the
  bed"; measured gap between families: bedding 0.79-1.0 vs riders 0.02-0.24).
* `carry_ons` strip rule — riders leave by geometry, two printed reasons
  (flanking cabinet / backdrop panelling), never by name.
* `flat_accents_on_bank` gained a **head term** (0.45 m) — the flatness test
  alone ate two of this bed's lying pillows; what separates a lying pillow from
  an accent band is that the bank presses the head (0.02-0.29 m) and the plaid
  lies 0.70 m out on the field.
* `_place_bed_frame` fit: **s = min(cluster reach, field containment)** — the
  soft field meets the drawn rectangle, never overflows it. Reproduces both
  f52472c1's 0.68 and this bed's honest 0.903 from one premise.
* **FUSED CORE** classification — this bed models frame+mattress as one shell
  with the duvet spread full-width; the first cut named the duvet the mattress
  (ratchet would have counted the whole made surface as bare core). A mattress
  rests on its base; cloth falls past it — the floor term (`lo z`) separates
  them. Under-bed slabs dress as FRAME, not bedding (z term on the bedding cut).
* `model_assert_check.SHELVES` + `_model_path` gained the blenderkit shelf in
  the same edit (two consumers, one shelf list).
* 7 new tests pin all of it (`test_wholebed_rules.py`, 43 pass).
