## Two of my own mechanisms died in this round

- **COUNT-TO-FIT for garments — killed by the R5 playblast, one hour old.** I removed
  the run term from the garment scale and culled garments to fit instead. The quick
  frame rendered a wardrobe of **bare hangers**. The reason is in the build's own log
  and I had not read it: `cce50840` stacks its four garments FRONT TO BACK
  (*"depth-axis fallback → 4 garment plane(s) on axis x"*), so every cluster occupies
  the same run and culling cannot shrink the row. Reverted, with the finding written
  at the constant.
- **THE DROP FIX IS CORRECT AND MEASURED INERT.** Deriving the clear drop from the
  wardrobe instead of from styling's placeholder is right — 1005 mm against 847, and
  it matches D4-A's signed short-hang. With the run term restored, `s_fit` comes back
  **0.785 / 0.728 / 0.742 / 0.774 / 0.813 / 0.756 / 0.698 / 0.802 / 0.745 —
  identical to p2r47 on all nine rails.** A latent defect is gone; not one pixel moved.
