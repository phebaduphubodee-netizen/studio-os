instruments were green at once: `cloth_edge` 0.686× (pass), mask-scoped fold energy

**the eye wins on WHETHER something is wrong; the instruments only say how much and

**What the measurement then said, once it was pointed at the right question.** Five

## 3. Two of my own claims were refuted mid-round, both by probing instead of shipping

**"The duvet's albedo knob is 88% dead."** An affine fit to two points put a sheen floor
at sRGB 176 against a target of 181, which would have meant the target was unreachable.

**167.9**, below that floor. The floor was an artefact of two close points. Had I shipped
the conclusion I would have written a "knob that cannot reach" finding into the record
about a knob that reaches fine.

**The probe method itself was controlled before it was believed.** `bed__mattress` was
on the same tone in the full frame and in the playblast and read **136.3** vs **136.5** —

## 4. One check was narrowed, and here is the test that makes it a correction

COLLAPSE fired on the acquired ottoman and the foot throw at 9.5 codes. Those two are
**one cloth by signature** (D3-4; the throw wears `bed_base`'s rgba), so no tone can
separate them — the built bench cleared the throw by 41 codes through LIGHT and position,
and that relationship left with it at p2r38. COLLAPSE now compares rungs on DIFFERENT
tones; same-tone pairs still print their gap as a composition note.

**A narrowing is a loophole unless it still catches what the instrument was built for.**
Both historical defects are cross-tone and both still fail: the founding one (six pieces
of the head inside 7.1 codes, spanning `duvet_set` and `pillowcase`) and the one he
rejected by eye at p2r41 (duvet 208.3 against pillow 217.2). Pinned in
`test_the_narrowing_still_catches_the_defect_the_ladder_was_built_for`. D-078.

Separately, the old ORDER check was carrying two claims in one branch — a squeeze and an
inversion — and the test named "catches an inversion" turned out to encode a squeeze. They
are now COLLAPSE and ORDER, and the real inversion has its own test.

## 6. What was decided, and the one item next

D-075 owner · D-076 the ladder blocks · D-077 acquired rungs resolve through `wears` ·
D-078 COLLAPSE narrowed to cross-tone with its own loophole test · D-079 solve the tone,
never the target. All five print in the render path on every gate run.

**NEXT, and it is one item, named by three independent judges including his:** the bed
cloth's SURFACE, not its value. `bed_throw` measures 0.577× of delivered work's weave
energy on its own pixels while the frame's overall octave structure passes — so this is
a per-cloth surface question, not a frame question. Two things are worth doing in one
round because they are the same mechanism: the throw and duvet carry sheen 0.7–0.8 on a
material D3-2 signs as **stonewashed linen**, while the same signed cloth family is
authored at 0.2 and 0.3 elsewhere — **four sheen values for one identity, typed at four
call sites, with nothing owning the parameter.** That is exactly the defect the tonal
ladder was created to end, one parameter over. Sheen also has a measured effect on the
ladder (γ falls from 1.11 at sheen 0.2 to 0.22 at sheen 0.7), so it must move through
the ladder, not beside it.

**Open and said plainly:** the duvet still sits at 0.737 of frame p99 against delivered
work's 0.51–0.67, because its signed target of 181 is itself above that band. Moving it
is a legitimate re-anchor — to the ANCHOR POOL (R4), not to our own render — and it is a
separate decision from this round's, so it is not smuggled in here.
