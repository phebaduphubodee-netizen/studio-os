## 1. THE FINDING OF THE ROUND

C2 is a fresh-context local agent handed exactly two files: the render and the
standing prompt. No spec, no scene dump, no build log, no round number. Its
item §3(a):

> *"Roughly **40 %** of the stack projects past the bench's rolled corner into
> open air — you can see the grey bed behind the overhanging half. Centre of
> mass over fresh air; that stack falls."*

Measured from the scene dump afterwards: the book stack spans 155.0 mm in y, of
which **60.4 mm — 39.0 % — hangs past the ottoman.**

**And the cause is a defect I introduced this morning, inside the fix for its own
twin.** p2r38 found that the bench's books sat at a TYPED z off the spec's
declared height, which would bury them in a 501 mm ottoman standing in a 450 mm
slot. I derived the TOP from the mesh actually in the scene and **left the PLAN on
the drawn slot.** An acquired mesh fills its slot to `model_fit`'s MIN_FILL, not
to the millimetre: `Ottoman_01` lands **498 × 709 in a 498 × 1000 drawn slot**, so
books placed 85 mm from the SLOT's near edge began 60 mm before the ottoman did.

Same defect. One axis away. Fixed the same day it shipped, and the fix is
verified rather than asserted: **39.0 % → 0.0 %** on the p2r40 playblast.

R9's law is not "derive the z". It is *a position that can be derived from a
contact must never be typed* — and x and y are positions.

## 4. WHAT THE TWO CRITICS TOGETHER SAY ABOUT THIS ROUND'S OWN DECISION

I wrote into the p2r38 gate, before either critic saw anything, that I would not
sign `Ottoman_01`'s FORM, and that my PBR-map discriminator might be wrong for
upholstery. The ladder came back with a sharper answer than my doubt:

- **Neither critic named the form.** Not "traditional", not "bun feet", not
  "wrong style". Two independent designers looked and did not see what I saw.
  That is evidence against my own read, and it is worth recording as such.
- **Both named the SURFACE**, which is the half I flagged as uncertain.
- And the measurement then refuted the surface item as *stated* (1.82× our own
  woven linen's relief) while leaving its sheen and compression halves standing.

**So the real defect is the one neither I nor the critics named directly, and it
falls out of C2 §3f's refutation:** the drawn bench is **498 × 1000, an aspect of
1:2.0**, and **no cached candidate is that shape.** Ottoman_01 is 1:1.42 and fills
709 of the drawn 1000 mm. The alternatives invert it — `e73605b0` and `250b8e09`
fit the length and fill only ~350 of the drawn 498 mm depth. `MIN_FILL = 0.62`
permits all of them, which is exactly what it is for, and the consequence is that
a 1000 mm drawn bench became a 709 mm pouf covering a third of the bed's width.

**That is a SOURCING GAP and it is now declared rather than absorbed.** It is not
an argument for loosening MIN_FILL, and not an argument for stretching a mesh.
