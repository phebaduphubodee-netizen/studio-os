**Finish locked (my call, A4):** **PVD-coated satin/brushed brass** — PVD resists
spotting/tarnish under constant water; raw/living-finish brass is ruled out.

**⭐ Coherence rule (the one design call that matters here):** pick **ONE brand +
one finish name** across *all* wet-room brass (2 basin taps, shower valve, rain head,
handheld, bath filler, towel rail, hooks, health-faucet). Mixing brands makes the
brass *hue* clash — the market has a **warm** brushed brass (Kohler *Moderne Brass*,
Hansgrohe/AXOR *Brushed Brass*, warm-gold tones) and a **cool** one (Grohe *Brushed
Cool Sunrise*). The suite's existing accent is **warm satin brass** (bedside dome
lamps) → **stay in the WARM family** so the ensuite reads as the same suite. This
rules Grohe Cool Sunrise *out on tone*, premium build notwithstanding.

## Tiers (all warm brushed brass unless noted)

### Tier A — signature-grade (premium, best finish integrity)
- **Hansgrohe / AXOR — "Brushed Brass"** (PVD). Warm, the most tone-coherent with the
  suite's satin brass; full range covers every piece above. Premium build + warranty.
- Channel: Boonthavorn, Hafele TH, authorized Hansgrohe dealers.
- **Indicative package: ~฿120,000–200,000+** (basin mixers ~฿15–25k ea, thermostatic
  shower system ~฿45–75k, bath filler ~฿20–35k, accessories ~฿10–20k).

### Tier B — mid-premium (recommended sweet spot)
- **Kohler — "Vibrant Brushed Moderne Brass"** (Vibrant = PVD, tarnish/corrosion
  resistant per Kohler). Warm moderne brass, coheres with the suite. Collections
  *Composed™ / Parallel™ / Artifacts™*. **Caveat:** the brushed-moderne-brass finish
  is offered on a *limited* set of models in TH (kohler.co.th showed it on ~1 basin
  SKU) — confirm each piece is available in that finish, or fall back to **Vibrant
  French Gold** (polished, wider range) for any missing piece (note: French Gold is
  *polished* not brushed → a finish-family split; prefer all-brushed if the range allows).
- Channel: shop.kohler.co.th e-shop, Boonthavorn, HomePro.
- **Indicative package: ~฿70,000–120,000.**

### Tier C — value (Thai-domestic, finish-match compromise)
- **American Standard TH — "Polished Warm Sunset"** on *Signature™ / Acacia Supasleek™
  / Cygnet™*. A warm gold tone BUT **polished, not brushed** (flag: reads shinier,
  shows spots more than a satin PVD, less premium). Strong Thai support + spares.
- Or **COTTO / Karat brushed-gold** lines (HomePro / Thaiwatsadu), cheapest.
- Channel: HomePro, Thaiwatsadu, americanstandard.co.th. AS basin faucets **฿529–5,090**
  (up to ฿14,890 for premium mixers); generic brushed-gold rain sets from Thaiwatsadu.
- **Indicative package: ~฿25,000–55,000.**

## Recommendation
**Tier B (Kohler Vibrant Brushed Moderne Brass)** for a master suite: PVD satin +
warm tone that matches the suite's brass, at roughly half Tier A's cost — *provided*
the needed pieces exist in that finish (the one thing to confirm on a quote). If the
range has gaps, **Tier A (Hansgrohe/AXOR Brushed Brass)** is the finish-safe fallback.
Tier C only if budget forces it, accepting the polished-vs-brushed compromise.
